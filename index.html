Research

Current research

Neural quantum states for anyons

I am developing variational neural-network wave functions for Abelian anyons in two-dimensional harmonic traps. The goal is to encode particle statistics and exchange symmetry directly into the ansatz while retaining a numerically stable variational Monte Carlo formulation.

Self-attention · Jastrow transformation · variational Monte Carlo

Quantum Monte Carlo for spin–valley ferromagnetism

I use stochastic-series-expansion quantum Monte Carlo to study finite-temperature spin–valley physics in rhombohedral multilayer graphene, with emphasis on BKT behavior, helicity-modulus estimators, and finite-size scaling.

SSE QMC · directed-loop updates · helicity modulus · BKT scaling

Neural quantum states for electron–hole bilayers

I am developing self-attention neural quantum states for periodic electron–hole bilayers, with a current focus on imbalanced Ne = 2Nh systems relevant to trion physics. The ansatz combines periodic features, separate electron and hole Slater determinants, a bilayer Jastrow factor, and variational Monte Carlo optimization.

PsiFormer · periodic boundary conditions · Slater–Jastrow · VMC / KFAC
