Research Notes

Two longer-form notes paired with the code projects on this site: one on the stochastic-series-expansion QMC algorithm and one on the black-hole information paradox through holographic entanglement calculations.

Planned technical note

Stochastic-series-expansion quantum Monte Carlo

A step-by-step introduction to the SSE formulation used in my spin–valley calculations. The emphasis will be on how the algorithm is actually constructed: from the high-temperature expansion and operator-string representation to linked vertices, Monte Carlo updates, and estimators for thermodynamic observables.

  • Partition-function expansion and the operator-string configuration space
  • Bond decomposition, diagonal updates, and the no-branching basis
  • Directed-loop updates and open-walk updates when transverse fields break spin conservation
  • Counting estimators, winding numbers, and the helicity modulus
  • Binning, finite-size analysis, and BKT transition-temperature extraction

Planned explanatory note

Black-hole information paradox through holographic entanglement entropy

An accessible route from holographic entanglement entropy to the Page curve and island prescription. The note will focus on how competing Ryu–Takayanagi / quantum-extremal surfaces are calculated and how the same framework changes in AdS/BCFT, codimension-two brane, higher-curvature, and cone-holography settings.

  • Entanglement entropy, extremal surfaces, and generalized entropy
  • The Page curve and why semiclassical Hawking radiation creates an information problem
  • Island saddles and competition between extremal surfaces
  • Codimension-two branes, AdS/BCFT, curvature-squared gravity, and cone holography
  • Numerical strategies for time-dependent extremal-surface calculations
