Spin–valley SSE quantum Monte Carlo

Simulation and analysis code for stochastic-series-expansion quantum Monte Carlo studies of finite-temperature spin–valley local-moment models in rhombohedral multilayer graphene.

  • Operator-string representation and model-specific bond decompositions
  • Diagonal, directed-loop, and open-walk update routines
  • Counting estimators, winding numbers, and helicity-modulus measurements
  • Binning, parameter sweeps, finite-size scaling, fitting, and visualization
